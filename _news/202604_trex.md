[TREX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14116) technical report is available. We introduce TREX, a tree-search enhanced agentic system that automates the challenging process of LLM fine-tuning, along with a new benchmark, [FT-Bench](https://github.com/trex-project/ftbench), comprising 10 fine-tuning tasks to evaluate automatic research systems. Check out the [project page](/projects/trex/) for more information.
