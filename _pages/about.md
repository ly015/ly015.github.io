I am a Research Scientist at [Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory](https://www.shlab.org.cn) and a member of the [Intern Large Models](https://huggingface.co/internlm) team, where I lead research on agentic coding and LLM post-training. My work focuses on training-data pipelines and model fine-tuning for coding agents tackling software engineering and terminal tasks. I led coding post-training for Atria Dawn and co-led supervised fine-tuning for InternLM 2/2.5/3. I also work on autonomous AI R&D and serve as an adjunct Ph.D. supervisor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Engineering from [Multimedia Lab (MMLab)](https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2019, advised by [Prof. Chen Change Loy](https://www.mmlab-ntu.com/person/ccloy/) and [Prof. Xiaoou Tang](https://www.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/faculty/Tang-Xiaoou-Sean/), and my B.S. in Automation from Tsinghua University in 2014. Before joining Shanghai AI Lab, I was a Research Scientist at SenseTime from 2019 to 2021. I previously served as a core maintainer of [OpenMMLab](https://github.com/open-mmlab) and led the development of [MMPose](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmpose).
